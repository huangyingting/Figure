# Figure Learning

Figure turns a subject into an illustrated lesson that learners can review, organize, and practice.

## Language

**Figure**:
A saved lesson comprising an image and its editable annotation layer. A figure belongs to one owner and is private unless its owner publishes it.

**Component**:
A named part of the subject with an explanation and a location on the image. Its identity stays stable when its name, explanation, or placement changes.
_Avoid_: Label (the visible presentation, not the component itself)

**Annotation**:
The lesson title, summary, component inventory, spatial placements, and review warnings accompanying an image. Editing annotations does not change the image.

**Visual style**:
The artistic treatment of a figure, such as isometric, realistic, or blueprint. It is distinct from the structural diagram format: a cutaway or exploded view can use any of these treatments.

**Review status**:
The level of human review applied to a component: an AI draft, a human edit, or an explicit approval. Publication does not imply approval.

**Collection**:
A learner-owned grouping of accessible figures. Adding a figure does not copy it or transfer ownership.

**Favorite**:
A learner's bookmark of an accessible figure, independent of collections.

**Quiz attempt**:
A completed recall exercise on the visible components of one figure. Its answers and score describe that exercise, not a guarantee of future mastery.

**Credit**:
An allowance to generate one figure. A failed generation returns its consumed credit; editing, copying, collecting, and practicing do not consume credits.

**Credit ledger**:
The history explaining an account's credit balance. Each generation charge and its possible refund have a shared reference and occur at most once.
